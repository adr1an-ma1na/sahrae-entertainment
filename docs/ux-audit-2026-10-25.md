# User walk-through, 2026-10-25

Walked through as a viewer on a phone (375 px), a laptop (1024 px) and at TV width, in the web app at
`june-plus` f4c8c67. Each issue is listed with what a viewer sees, how bad it is, and what was done.
Major = something does not work, or blocks a viewer. Minor = it works but looks or reads wrong.

## Fixed

| # | Where | What the viewer saw | Severity | Fix |
|---|-------|--------------------|----------|-----|
| 1 | Live Sports, web | Tapping any match left "Loading admin · English…" spinning for ever. The feeds were added to the list, but the code then looked them up in a copy of the list from before they were added, so none was ever checked or loaded. | Major | The lookup now uses the new list (`SportsView.tsx`, `openEvent`). |
| 2 | Live Sports, web | With that fixed, every feed showed a red "Remove sandbox attributes on the iframe tag" screen. Ad Shield is on by default on the web, and the sports player (embed.st) refuses to run sandboxed. | Major | Players that refuse the sandbox now load without it (`adShield.ts`, `SANDBOX_REFUSERS`); the shield toggle is not offered for them. |
| 3 | Films and series, web | The same: VidRock ("Sandbox Not Allowed"), Viduki ("This player was sandboxed") and VidLink Pro ("Please Disable Sandbox") never played with the shield on. Only VidZee did. This is what "every server fails" looked like on the web: the player stepped through all four in seconds. | Major | Same list. The Ad Shield panel now says when a server does not allow it, instead of claiming it is on. Pinned by `sandbox-check.mjs`. |
| 4 | Live Sports | Feeds were tried in the provider's order. A feed four people were watching (an "offline" slate, checked: "flux hors ligne") could be offered before the one 10,700 people were watching. | Major | Feeds are ranked HD first, then by how many people are watching (`rankFeeds`, tested in `sportsstreams-check.mjs`). |
| 5 | Player full screen, web | On an iPhone, or anywhere the browser refuses element full screen, the full-screen button did nothing. When the full-window fallback had been entered another way, the button could not leave it. | Major | Falls back to filling the window; the same button, Esc or Back leaves it; the phone turns to landscape where allowed (`PlayerModal.tsx`, `fullscreen.ts`). |
| 6 | Player, phones | Turning the phone sideways did not fill the screen with the picture. | Minor | Turning to landscape fills the screen, turning back restores the page, in films, series and sports. |
| 7 | Live Sports full screen | No fallback either; same as 5. | Major | Same fallback, plus the rotate behaviour. |
| 8 | Search | The Search tab had filters but no box to type in. The only text field was hidden behind the top-bar icon, so on a phone there was no way to search by name. | Major | One search box at the top of the Search page, results as you type (`SearchBox.tsx`). The top-bar icon now opens that page (tablet and up; phones have the Search tab). |
| 9 | Search | No results showed an empty page with no explanation. | Minor | "Nothing found for …" with what to try, or a prompt when nothing has been typed. |
| 10 | Sign in | Anyone could use the app without an account. | Major (owner's request) | Sign-in is required: email and password, create account, reset password (`SignInGate.tsx`). Nothing plays until signed in. |
| 11 | Sign in, every launch | With sign-in required, the old start-up told the app "signed out" before the saved session was read, which would flash the sign-in screen at signed-in viewers. | Major | The app waits for the saved session (`firebase.ts`, `ready`). |
| 12 | Reset password | The reset link pointed at the site root (`adr1an-ma1na.github.io`, without `/sahrae-entertainment/`), and from the Android app at `https://localhost`, which an email cannot open. The app also ignored the link's sign-in. | Major | Links point at the web app; the app reads them and asks for the new password (`NewPasswordSheet`). |
| 13 | Errors at sign-in | Supabase's own wording ("Invalid login credentials"). | Minor | Plain messages for the common cases, in English and Kiswahili. |
| 14 | New account | A new account landed on an empty "Who's watching?" and had to build a profile before seeing anything. | Minor | The first profile is made from the account name and opens straight away. |
| 15 | Every launch | "Who's watching?" every time, even with one profile. | Minor | The last profile used on that device opens by itself; Switch Profile is in Settings. |
| 16 | Top bar, phone | Logo, bell, language, remote, theme and sign-in crowded a 375 px bar; the bell's dot sat on the logo. | Minor | Language and theme moved into Settings on phones (still in the bar from 640 px). |
| 17 | Bottom bar, phone | Live Sports was two taps away, inside More. | Minor | Sports has its own tab: Home, Search, Sports, Listen, More. |
| 18 | Live Sports player, phone | Title, LIVE, Ad Shield, Dead Server, full screen and close were all laid over a 375 px video and covered each other and the match. | Minor | On phones the title and buttons sit above the picture; over it only on larger screens and in full screen. One "Not playing?" button instead of two. |
| 19 | Live Sports player | Developer text: "admin · English", "health 50/100", "Not verifiable in a browser, playing unchecked", "cross-origin". | Minor | "Feed 1 · English · HD", "Live · HD", plain tips. |
| 20 | Player, film | The server menu read "Server 1…4". | Minor | It names the server (VidZee, VidRock…). |
| 21 | Player bar | Icon buttons had no names for screen readers. | Minor | Labelled. |
| 22 | Player | "Google AI Studio's preview window blocks…" shown when the app is framed by another site. | Minor | Neutral wording. |
